CMF iPro 2 OS Architecture

An Apple-inspired frosted UI customisation layer designed for the CMF Phone 2 Pro (Galaga A001). Non-destructive, lightweight, zero-firmware-flash.

Interactive Guide

  • Chassis Essential Key: Click the orange physical key on the left side of the phone frame to invoke Essential Space.
  • Control Center Sliders: Drag the Brightness and Volume vertical sliders up and down in real-time.
  • Theme & Blur Live Sync: Adjust the theme toggle and blur radius slider at the top to see instant GPU-accelerated frosted glass rendering.
  • Bottom Home Bar: Tap the home pill at the bottom to return to Home Screen from any screen.

Tier 3: Iconify Split Shade, Vertical Sliders, Custom Status icons
Tier 4: Companion Frosted Essential Space overlay drawer
